About Simon

Movement.
Challenge.
Skill progression.

After years of stress, burnout, and unhealthy habits, Simon made the biggest decision of his life. At the height of his career in construction management, he walked away from the corporate world to pursue what he'd come to see as his calling: helping other people ease that same stress and get moving in their bodies again.

He came to coaching with more than 12 years of fitness experience behind him, spanning bodybuilding, powerlifting, strength & conditioning training, traditional Chinese martial arts, lion dancing, boxing, Muay Thai, and kickboxing.

He is now a dedicated coach with a Certificate III & IV in Fitness. Specialising in kickboxing, he guides people through movement, challenge, and skill progression.

Simon loves to fill the sessions with laughter, high-energy music, and a sense of accomplishment. So you can expect the training experience to be built to bring joy, excitement, and a deeper belief in what you're capable of.
