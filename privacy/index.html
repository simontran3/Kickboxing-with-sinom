Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what personal information is collected when you book or train, why it is collected, and how you can get it changed or removed.

Who we are

Kickboxing with Simon is a sole trader business run by Simon Tran in Lidcombe, NSW. ABN 15 676 046 300.

Simon handles all personal information himself. There are no staff, and no other person has access to your details.

This policy is written in plain language and follows the Australian Privacy Principles.

How to contact us about privacy

For any question, correction, or request about your information, contact Simon directly.

Simon Tran

What is collected, and who collects it

Three services are used to run bookings, screening, and the website. Each one is listed below with exactly what it holds.

Calendly, used for booking sessions

When you book a session, Calendly collects your name, your email address, and your phone number. This is used to confirm the booking, send reminders, and contact you if a session needs to move.

Typeform, used for pre-exercise screening and the waiver

Before your first session you complete a screening and waiver form. It asks about injuries, medical conditions, medications, and anything else that affects safe training. Your answers, and your agreement to the waiver, are stored in Typeform.

Health information is sensitive information. Australian privacy law treats it with extra protection.

Simon collects it for one reason only, which is to run your sessions safely and adjust the training to suit you.

It is not used for marketing, it is not shared with anyone else, and it is not used to make decisions about you outside training.

Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager

The website measures how it is used. This covers pages viewed, buttons tapped, the approximate location worked out from your IP address, and basic device and browser details.

You do not need an account, and you are never asked to identify yourself. Simon sees group patterns, such as how many people tapped the booking button, rather than named individuals.

Why this information is collected

  • To run your bookings. Confirming times, sending reminders, and reaching you if something changes.
  • To keep you safe while training. Knowing about an injury, a heart condition, or a medication changes what Simon asks you to do.
  • To improve the website. Seeing which pages people read, and which buttons they use, shows what is worth fixing.

No information is collected for any purpose beyond these three.

Who your information is shared with

Your information is shared with the three services named above, because those services store it on Simon's behalf. Calendly holds booking details, Typeform holds screening answers, and Google holds website analytics.

Nobody else receives it. Your information is never sold, never rented, and never traded. It is not given to advertisers, other trainers, or gyms.

The only exception is where the law requires disclosure, for example a court order or a legal obligation Simon cannot refuse.

Where your information is stored

Calendly, Typeform, and Google are overseas providers, so some of your information is stored on servers outside Australia. Each provider is responsible for securing the information it holds under its own terms.

Simon also keeps his own records of bookings and screening answers on a password-protected personal computer in Australia. Nobody else has access to it.

How long it is kept

  • Booking details. Kept while you are training with Simon, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back.
  • Screening and waiver answers. Kept for seven years after your last session, and for a participant under 18, until they turn 25. This follows the retention period that applies to health records in New South Wales, and it is kept for risk management and insurance reasons.
  • Website analytics. Kept by Google under the retention setting on the analytics account, then deleted automatically.

If you ask for your information to be deleted, Simon will remove what he holds unless he is required to keep it.

Seeing, correcting, or deleting your information

You can ask to see what Simon holds about you, correct anything that is wrong, or have it deleted. Email simontran3@gmail.com or call 0408 785 125.

Simon will respond within a reasonable time and will not charge you for the request. If he cannot action it, he will explain why in writing.

Cookies and website analytics

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser through Google Tag Manager. Cookies are small files that let the site recognise a returning visit and count it correctly.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The website still works normally if you do either.

Making a complaint

If you think your privacy has been mishandled, contact Simon first so he can try to fix it. If you are not satisfied with the response, you can raise it with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the way information is collected changes. The current version, and the date it was last updated, always appear on this page.
