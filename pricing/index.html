Common questions

Before you book

Do I need my own gloves?

No. Gloves, pads, and the rest of the gear are supplied for every session. Bring water and clothes you can move in, and that is enough.

Can friends join a group session?

Yes. Family and friends are welcome, and group training is often easier for people who feel nervous about starting. Group sessions are $30 per person, per hour.

Where do sessions happen?

At the private gym in Lidcombe, or Simon travels to you. That can be your home, a local park, or another space that works for you.
