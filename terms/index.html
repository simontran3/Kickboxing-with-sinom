What is covered

  • Private one-on-one coaching. An hour of kickboxing training with Simon alone.
  • Group sessions. Training in a small group, with the work adjusted to suit each person.
  • Mobile personal training. Simon travels to your home, a local park, or another agreed space.

Equipment, including gloves and pads, is supplied by Simon for every session.

Booking and payment

Private sessions can be booked through Calendly or directly with Simon. Group classes are booked through Bookwhen. Your first session is free, so you can try the training before paying anything.

Group class: $30 per person, per class.

Private one-on-one: $120 per session, or $400 for a pack of four sessions.

Semi private, two people: $170 per session for the two of you, or $600 for a pack of four sessions.

Payment is due at or before each session. Simon will tell you which payment methods he accepts when you book.

Prices may change over time. Any change applies to sessions booked after the change, never to a session you have already booked.

Cancelling a session

If you need to cancel, give Simon at least 24 hours' notice and the session can be rescheduled at no cost.

Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice, or not showing up, means the session is charged in full. Simon will use his judgement where something genuinely unavoidable comes up, such as illness or a family emergency.

If Simon cancels a session, you can reschedule it or have it refunded, whichever you prefer.

Changing a session time

If you need to move a session, contact Simon directly on 0408 785 125 as early as you can. Simon will do the same if he needs to move a session.

Pre-exercise screening and waiver

You must complete the pre-exercise screening and waiver form before your first session. This is not optional, and training cannot start until it is done.

The form asks about injuries, medical conditions, medications, and anything else that affects safe training. Answer it honestly and in full, because Simon uses it to decide what is safe to ask of you.

Tell Simon if anything changes after you fill it in. A new injury, a new diagnosis, a pregnancy, or a change in medication all change what training is appropriate.

How your screening answers are stored and used is set out in the Privacy Policy.

Training carries risk

Kickboxing is physical exercise, and physical exercise carries a risk of injury. That includes sprains, strains, bruising, joint and muscle injury, and, in rare cases, more serious harm.

Simon takes reasonable care. He plans sessions to suit your fitness, teaches technique before intensity, supplies protective equipment, and adjusts the work when something is not right for you.

You are responsible for your part of that. Specifically, you agree to:

  • Disclose your health conditions, injuries, and medications in the screening form, and keep Simon updated.
  • Follow Simon's instructions during a session.
  • Stop, and tell Simon, if you feel pain, dizziness, or anything else that feels wrong.
  • Get medical advice before starting if you have any doubt about whether training is safe for you.

To the extent permitted by law, you take part in training at your own risk, and Simon is not liable for injury or loss that happens despite him taking reasonable care.
